Privacy Policy for TaskFlow

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskFlow ("we", "us", or "our") accesses, collects, uses, stores, shares, retains, and deletes personal information when you use our e-commerce application at https://taskflow-das.duckdns.org. By using TaskFlow, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

1. Information We Collect

Google user data

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides us with Google user data needed to create and manage your TaskFlow account. Depending on the Google permissions you approve, this may include your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier. We request only the basic Google scopes needed for sign-in: openid, email, and profile. We do not receive or store your Google password.

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: name, email address, password, phone number, and profile details.
  • Order information: products purchased, order history, billing/shipping address, delivery notes, and support messages.
  • Payment status and limited transaction details from Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Reviews, feedback, survey responses, and marketing preferences if you choose to provide them.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and referring URL.
  • Usage data such as pages visited, products viewed, items added to cart, timestamps, and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Cookies and local storage used to keep you signed in, remember your cart, and maintain security preferences.
  • Error and diagnostic logs used to troubleshoot and secure the service.

Information from third parties

  • Google account data when you use Google sign-in.
  • Payment processors may provide transaction status or fraud signals.
  • Shipping or fulfillment partners may provide delivery status updates.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information, including Google user data, to:

  • Create, maintain, and secure your account.
  • Authenticate you when you sign in with Google or email/password.
  • Identify you within our system to manage your cart, orders, and account history.
  • Process orders, payments, shipping, refunds, returns, and customer support requests.
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, receipts, email verification, password reset, and security alerts.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and other harmful activity.
  • Debug errors, improve performance, and understand how the service is used.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
  • Send optional marketing emails only if you have opted in.

We use Google user data only to provide or improve TaskFlow functionality that you request, such as signing you in and maintaining your account. We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing, selling data, or unrelated purposes.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy / Limited Use

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google user data only to authenticate you and provide TaskFlow features you request.
  • We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide the service, with your consent, for security or legal reasons, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data except with your consent, for security reasons, to comply with law, or for internal operations consistent with Google's policies.

4. How We Share Information

We may share personal information with:

  • Payment processors such as Stripe to complete transactions and prevent fraud.
  • Email delivery providers such as Resend to send transactional and service emails.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that operate the website and databases.
  • Shipping or fulfillment partners when needed to deliver your order.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants or lawyers when necessary.
  • Legal authorities or other parties when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety.
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

We may also share aggregated or anonymized data that cannot reasonably identify you. We do not sell personal information.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember your cart, store security tokens, and remember preferences. You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings, but some features may stop working.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information, including Google user data, only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Typical retention periods include:

  • Account data is kept while your account is active and for a limited period after deletion.
  • Order and transaction records may be kept longer to meet tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, and legal obligations.
  • Security and diagnostic logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted or anonymized.
  • Email verification, password reset, and sign-in tokens expire automatically.

If you want to delete your account and associated personal data, including Google user data, contact us at souravdas090300@gmail.com. We will verify your request and delete or anonymize the data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted HTTPS connections, access controls, secure token handling, and database security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
